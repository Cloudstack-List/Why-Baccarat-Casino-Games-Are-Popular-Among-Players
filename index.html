How the Game Works

A standard round follows a simple structure where two hands are dealt: one for the Player and one for the Banker. Each hand receives two to three cards, and the main goal is to reach a total closest to nine. Card values are fixed, with face cards counted as zero and Aces counted as one.

Betting Options

Players can choose between three main betting options: Player, Banker, or Tie. Each option offers different payout possibilities, but the Banker side is often preferred by more experienced players due to its slightly lower house edge.
